你的数据与你的选择

设备内保存

修为专注是一款离线专注计时 App。功课、计时记录、修行进度、杂念、心得与偏好保存在你的设备上。当前版本没有账号、广告、分析 SDK、业务服务器或 App 内云同步，不会自动将这些内容发送给开发者，也不会在不同设备或浏览器预览之间自动同步。

声音与通知

白噪音和自然声由设备本地合成，不下载音频，也不传输收听记录。倒计时结束提醒由系统在本机安排；通知权限可拒绝或关闭，不影响计时。心流需要手动结束，没有到期提醒。

备份与恢复副本

你可以导出或合并导入本机数据，单份导出与导入文件的上限均为 20 MB（20,000,000 字节）。导出文件含功课、记录与心得等内容，请妥善保管。生成备份后需要主动选择分享或保存位置；数据变化后须重新导出。

无法读取或识别旧数据时，App 会尽可能保留未修复的原始文件副本。设备内文件日期仅用于区分、排序恢复副本，不会传给开发者。普通备份只含当前可见数据，故障原件需通过“导出原始文件”另行留存；副本不表示数据已经恢复。仅有原件而当前数据为空时，普通导出会暂停，避免空白文件误作完整备份，请先单独导出原件。

文件由你决定保存或分享给谁。所选邮件、文件保存或分享服务如何处理文件，取决于该服务的政策；这不属于 App 自动同步。合并规则与恢复步骤见使用帮助。

清除与移除 App

“清除本机数据”只重置当前功课、修行记录、进度与设置，确认后无法撤销；设备内已保护的恢复原件会保留，仍可到“数据恢复”逐份导出。

如需移除 App 私有数据，请先保存需要的文件，再在系统中选择“删除 App”并删除其数据。iOS“卸载 App”（Offload）可能保留文稿与数据。App 不会自动删除你在外部另存或分享的备份与原件，请在相应位置自行管理。

联系与变更

隐私问题可发送至下方支持邮箱。你主动联系时，邮件中选择提供的信息会通过所用邮件服务发送给开发者；请仅提供必要说明，避免附上含私人内容的完整备份。功能或数据处理方式变化时，本政策会相应更新。

Xiuwei Focus · Privacy Policy

Updated: 24 October 2026

On-device storage

Xiuwei Focus is an offline focus timer. Tasks, session records, progress, captured thoughts, reflections and preferences stay on your device. The current version has no account, advertising, analytics SDK, backend server or in-app cloud sync. It does not automatically send this content to the developer or sync it between devices or the browser preview.

Sound and notifications

Ambient noise and nature sounds are generated on the device. The app does not download audio or transmit listening records. Countdown completion reminders are scheduled locally by the system. You can decline or disable notification permission without affecting the timer. Flow sessions must be ended manually and have no expiry reminder.

Backups and recovery copies

You can export or merge local data. Each exported or imported file is limited to 20 MB (20,000,000 bytes). Backups include tasks, records and reflections, so keep them securely. After generating a backup, you choose where to share or save it. Export again after data changes.

When old data cannot be read or recognized, the app tries to preserve unrepaired original files. On-device file dates are used only to distinguish and order recovery copies; they are not sent to the developer. An ordinary backup contains only currently visible data. Damaged originals must be saved separately through Export Original File. A recovery copy does not mean recovery has succeeded. When only originals remain and current data is empty, ordinary export is blocked to avoid a misleading empty backup; export the originals separately first.

You decide where files are saved and with whom they are shared. File handling by your chosen email, storage or sharing service is governed by that service’s policy; this is not automatic app sync. See Support for merge rules and recovery steps.

Clearing and removing the app

Clear Local Data resets only the current tasks, session records, progress and settings. Once confirmed, this reset cannot be undone. Protected recovery originals on the device are retained and can still be exported individually from Data Recovery.

To remove the app’s private data, first save any files you need, then use Delete App and delete its data in system settings. iOS Offload App may retain documents and data. The app does not automatically delete backups or originals you saved or shared elsewhere; manage them at their destinations.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, use the support address below. If you contact the developer, the information you choose to include is sent through your email service. Provide only necessary details and avoid attaching a full backup containing private content. This policy will be updated when features or data handling change.
